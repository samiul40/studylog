Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 12 September 2026

StudyLog ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to you when using StudyLog. StudyLog is the data controller for the information described here.

It should be read alongside our Terms and Conditions.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide

When you create an account or use StudyLog, we may collect:

  • Name or username
  • Email address
  • Password (stored only as a salted cryptographic hash — we never store or see your actual password)
  • Study logs and learning records
  • Goals, notes, and other content you choose to save
  • Feature suggestions you submit
  • Communications you send to us
  • Your timezone, so dates and times display correctly
  • A record of when you accepted our Terms and this Policy, which version you accepted, and your confirmation that you are 13 or over

Information From Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive your email address and name from Google. We never receive your Google password.

Information Collected Automatically

We automatically collect a limited amount of technical information:

  • IP addresses and usernames associated with failed sign-in attempts, used to detect and block brute-force attacks
  • Technical details of application errors, which may incidentally include your account identifier

We do not use advertising trackers, we do not run third-party analytics, we do not build profiles of your browsing, and we do not collect special category data.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Create and manage your account
  • Provide the StudyLog service
  • Save and display your study data
  • Send service emails such as account confirmation and password resets
  • Improve the platform and user experience
  • Respond to enquiries and support requests
  • Maintain security and prevent abuse
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We do not send marketing emails, and we will not start without asking you first.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable under UK data protection laws, we process your personal information based on:

  • Performance of our contract with you — providing the service, storing your study data, and sending service emails
  • Our legitimate interests in operating, securing and improving StudyLog
  • Compliance with legal obligations — including keeping a record of when you accepted our terms
  • Your consent, where we rely on it

4. Data Storage and Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, hashed passwords, protection against brute-force sign-in attempts, and access controls that keep your study data visible only to you.

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Data Retention

We keep your account and study data for as long as your account is open.

If you delete your account, it is deactivated immediately and permanently erased after 30 days. During that window you can reactivate it by signing in again. After 30 days the data cannot be recovered.

If you never confirm your email address, your account cannot be used, and we delete it 30 days after sign-up.

We may retain information for longer where required to comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use a small number of service providers who process data on our behalf, under our instructions:

  • Resend — delivers our account and service emails.
  • Google — provides the optional "Sign in with Google" feature, and the YouTube Data API used to look up video details when you add a YouTube resource.
  • Sentry — collects technical error reports so we can diagnose and fix faults.
  • Our hosting and cloud infrastructure providers.

We may also disclose information where required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or security of StudyLog, our users, or others.

7. Cookies

We use a small number of strictly necessary cookies:

  • A session cookie, which keeps you signed in. It expires after two weeks.
  • A CSRF cookie, which protects forms against cross-site request forgery.

These are required for the site to work and cannot be switched off, so we do not show a cookie consent banner. We do not use advertising, profiling or analytics cookies. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings, although you will not be able to stay signed in if you do.

8. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Have inaccurate information corrected.
  • Request deletion of your personal information.
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities.
  • Receive your personal information in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's supervisory authority for data protection.

You can exercise your rights by contacting us using the details below. We aim to respond to requests within one month where required by law.

9. Children's Privacy

StudyLog is not intended for children under the age of 13, and you must confirm that you are at least 13 to create an account. If you are under 18, you should make sure you have permission from a parent or guardian where required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that such information has been collected, we will take reasonable steps to remove it. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us.

10. Third-Party Services

StudyLog may contain links to third-party websites or services — including the learning resources you choose to save.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of those third parties. We encourage you to review their privacy policies separately.

11. International Transfers

Some of our service providers may process or store information outside the UK.

Where that happens, we take reasonable measures to ensure appropriate safeguards are in place, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses, or an adequacy decision.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page together with an updated "Last Updated" date. We will tell you about significant changes through the website or by email where appropriate.

13. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact:

Email: studyloguk@gmail.com

By using StudyLog, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
