FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is StudyLog free?

Yes, StudyLog is currently free to use. You can create an account and start tracking your learning immediately — no credit card needed.

Who can use StudyLog?

StudyLog is built for anyone learning something new — students, developers, professionals and self-learners alike.

What can I track with StudyLog?

You can track courses, books, videos, tutorials, PDFs and any other learning resource you want to complete. Break each one into units and tick them off as you go.

How is StudyLog different from a todo list?

Unlike a normal todo list, StudyLog focuses on long-term learning progress. You can organise resources into units, track completion and see how your learning improves over time.

Is StudyLog available as an app?

StudyLog works directly in your browser and can be added to your phone home screen like an app — full-screen, on iOS and Android, with no app store download.
