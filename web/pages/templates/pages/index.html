How it works

Three steps to a finished course.

STEP 01

Add a resource

Drop in a YouTube playlist, a Udemy course, or a book. StudyLog holds them all side by side.

STEP 02

Break it into units

Split each resource into units — lectures, chapters, sessions — so the finish line feels reachable.

STEP 03

Tick & track

Check off units as you go. Your dashboard tallies the minutes, streaks and completion for you.
